# Film LUTs: sources and credits

The `.lut` files here are converted, unchanged in content, from the G'MIC film emulation
collection as published at https://github.com/YahiaAngelo/Film-Luts (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2024 Yahia).
The collection gathers the G'MIC film emulation presets (Pat David and contributors) and the
RawTherapee Film Simulation collection; the Fuji X-Trans III set was contributed to RawTherapee by
Stuart Sowerby. Some sources state CC BY-SA 4.0 for the RawTherapee collection: attribution is given
here and the converted files are shared under the same terms.

Film and camera names (Kodak, Fujifilm, Ilford, Agfa, Polaroid, Rollei, Lomography and their product
names) are trademarks of their owners. Obscura is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. The names
are used here only to describe which film each LUT emulates, in a personal build.

Conversion: tools/import_film_luts.py (13^3 grid kept as is, 16-bit).
